About Me

A Little About Me

An educator dedicated to shaping confident, capable business professionals through knowledge, discipline, and practical learning.

I am an Assistant Professor of BBA at Government Degree College, Indora, with a deep commitment to business education and student success. My academic background in business administration and management has shaped my approach to teaching, where I blend theoretical foundations with practical insights.

My interests span accounting, international business, management principles, and professional development. I strive to create an engaging learning environment that encourages curiosity, ethical business practices, and effective communication.

Beyond the classroom, I am dedicated to continuous learning and competitive examination preparation, believing that education is a lifelong journey that empowers both educators and students alike.
